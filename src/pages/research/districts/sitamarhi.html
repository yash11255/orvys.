Development Context

Socio-Economic & CSR Profile

Sitamarhi is designated as one of NITI Aayog's 13 Aspirational Districts in Bihar. With a population of 34.2 Lakh, Sitamarhi is prioritized for rapid transformation across health, nutrition, education, agriculture, and skilling. In FY 2023-24, CSR spend was ₹2.74 Cr.

Investment Opportunities

Priority CSR Interventions

Model Anganwadi Centres (Nand Ghar Model)

Upgrading rural Anganwadi centres with child-friendly learning kits, clean sanitation, solar lighting, and fortified nutrition supplementation.

Foundational Learning & STEM Classrooms

Remedial learning camps (Teaching at the Right Level), smart digital classrooms, and teacher capacity development in upper-primary schools.

Youth Skilling & Employment Linkages

Market-aligned vocational training in district ITIs with placement guarantees in logistics, retail, and manufacturing.

Orvys Perspective

Strategic Assessment

With strong district monitoring mechanisms under the Aspirational Districts Programme, CSR projects in Sitamarhi benefit from administrative backing and high accountability.
