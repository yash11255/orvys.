Socio-Economic Profile

Development Context & Need

Buxar is an agrarian district in Bihar with a population of 17.1 Lakh. In FY 2023-24, the district recorded ₹0.34 Cr in CSR expenditures, ranking #32 across the state's 38 districts with a per-capita spend of ₹2.0.

Orvys Perspective

Strategic Assessment

With modest current CSR inflows, Buxar offers corporate funders high visibility and measurable additionality for medium-sized (₹50L - ₹2 Cr) multi-year programmes.
