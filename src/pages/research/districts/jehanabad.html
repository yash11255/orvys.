Development Context

Socio-Economic & CSR Profile

Jehanabad is an agrarian district in Bihar with a population of 11.3 Lakh. In FY 2023-24, the district recorded ₹8.49 Cr in CSR expenditures, ranking #9 across the state's 38 districts with a per-capita spend of ₹75.4.

Investment Opportunities

Priority CSR Interventions

Primary Health Centre (PHC) Strengthening

Maternal-child healthcare wings, neonatal care stabilization, diagnostic kits, and 24/7 solar backup for rural health sub-centres.

Women's Self-Help Group (SHG) Enterprises

Revolving enterprise funds, modern packaging machinery, and market linkages for JEEViKA-affiliated women's collectives.

Digital Literacy & Secondary Education

Solar-powered computer labs in rural girls' high schools and career counseling networks to curb female dropout rates.

Orvys Perspective

Strategic Assessment

With modest current CSR inflows, Jehanabad offers corporate funders high visibility and measurable additionality for medium-sized (₹50L - ₹2 Cr) multi-year programmes.
