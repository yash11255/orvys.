The rules

What the law says about CSR in sports

  • CSR can fund sports. Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 lists "training to promote rural sports, nationally recognised sports, Paralympic sports and Olympic sports" as an eligible CSR activity.
  • Sports is a State subject. Under the Constitution, sports falls in the State List, so stadiums and academies are built and run by the Bihar government's sports department. CSR works best in partnership with it, not around it.
  • The Centre is inviting companies in. In January 2025 the Union Sports Minister asked companies to adopt one sport each and become thematic partners for Khelo India, indigenous sports, para sports and women's sports.
  • Infrastructure needs operations. A stadium without coaches, equipment and upkeep stays empty. CSR can fund the people and programmes that make infrastructure work.

Why invest now

Why PSUs and companies should back sport in Bihar

Momentum

Bihar hosted Khelo India Youth Games for the first time in 2025 and climbed from 28th to 14th. The state is now building block-level sports infrastructure.

A national call

The Union Sports Ministry has asked companies to adopt a sport and partner on Khelo India, para sports and women's sports.

Local mandate

PSUs with Bihar operations, such as NTPC and Indian Oil, can direct local-area CSR to sport in their home districts.

Youth dividend

Sport builds health, discipline and pathways to jobs for one of India's youngest populations.
