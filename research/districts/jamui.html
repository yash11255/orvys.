Socio-Economic Profile

Development Context & Need

Jamui is designated as one of NITI Aayog's 13 Aspirational Districts in Bihar. With a population of 17.6 Lakh, Jamui is prioritized for rapid transformation across health, nutrition, education, agriculture, and skilling. In FY 2023-24, CSR spend was ₹4.49 Cr.

Orvys Perspective

Strategic Assessment

With strong district monitoring mechanisms under the Aspirational Districts Programme, CSR projects in Jamui benefit from administrative backing and high accountability.

Investment Opportunities

Priority CSR Interventions in Jamui

High-impact development pathways aligned with Schedule VII and Jamui's specific deficits.

Priority Sector

Model Anganwadi Centres (Nand Ghar Model)

Upgrading rural Anganwadi centres with child-friendly learning kits, clean sanitation, solar lighting, and fortified nutrition supplementation.

Priority Sector

Foundational Learning & STEM Classrooms

Remedial learning camps (Teaching at the Right Level), smart digital classrooms, and teacher capacity development in upper-primary schools.

Priority Sector

Youth Skilling & Employment Linkages

Market-aligned vocational training in district ITIs with placement guarantees in logistics, retail, and manufacturing.
