Socio-Economic Profile

Development Context & Need

Saharsa lies within Bihar's northern riparian plains, where riverine flooding regularly disrupts livelihoods, primary healthcare, and schooling for its 19.0 Lakh residents. In FY 2023-24, Saharsa received ₹0.32 Cr in total CSR (₹1.7 per capita).

Orvys Perspective

Strategic Assessment

CSR capital in Saharsa is best deployed in recurring climate resilience mechanisms and farmer-producer aggregation rather than one-off relief distributions.

Investment Opportunities

Priority CSR Interventions in Saharsa

High-impact development pathways aligned with Schedule VII and Saharsa's specific deficits.

Priority Sector

Early Warning & Community Disaster Readiness

Village-level disaster response equipment, safe evacuation shelters with solar power, and community first-responder volunteer training.

Priority Sector

Makhana & Aquatic Livelihood Value Chains

Post-harvest processing clusters, direct farmer aggregation hubs, and SHG enterprise support for wetland agro-produce.

Priority Sector

Sanitation & Clean Drinking Water

High-plinth twin-pit community toilets and chlorination systems to prevent waterborne epidemic outbreaks post-inundation.
