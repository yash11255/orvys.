State Trend

Bihar CSR, FY 2020-21 to FY 2023-24

Total CSR in Bihar grew 2.9× in four years, from ₹89.89 Cr to ₹260.53 Cr. Despite this growth, Bihar still receives only ~1% of India's total state-attributed CSR despite accounting for 8.6% of the national population.

PSU vs Private Split

Who funds Bihar's CSR?

Private companies now contribute 65% of Bihar's CSR (₹169.40 Cr in FY24). PSU contributions peaked in FY23 at ₹110.29 Cr and stood at ₹91.13 Cr in FY24.

District Concentration

Top 20 districts by CSR (FY 2023-24)

Muzaffarpur and Patna alone accounted for 35% of Bihar's total CSR in FY24. Gold bars denote NITI Aayog Aspirational Districts. Dashed line indicates state average (₹6.86 Cr).

Per-Capita Disparity

CSR per person — where the gap is widest

Per-capita CSR (₹ per person, FY24) highlights districts that remain severely under-funded relative to their demographics. Bihar average stands at ₹23.5 per person, compared to the India national average of ₹223.
