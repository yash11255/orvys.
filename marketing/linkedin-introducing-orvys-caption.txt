Introducing Orvys.

India's companies spent ₹40,794 crore on CSR in FY 2024-25.
Bihar, home to 1 in 11 Indians, receives about 1% of it.

That gap is why Orvys exists.

We are a CSR advisory and implementation partner, rooted in Bihar and working across India. We help companies, PSUs and governments plan, deliver and measure CSR programmes where the need is greatest:

🔹 CSR strategy backed by district-level data
🔹 Programme design in nutrition, education, health, livelihoods and climate resilience
🔹 Vetted, CSR-1-registered implementing partners
🔹 Independent impact measurement, not just activity counts

Our first publication, the Bihar CSR Outlook Report 2026, maps four years of CSR across all 38 districts of Bihar, and sets out seven programmes ready to fund.
